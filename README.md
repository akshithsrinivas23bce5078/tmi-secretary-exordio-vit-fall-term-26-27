### Executive Committee (ExCom) Minutes of Meetings | Fall Term 2026–27

Welcome to the official repository for the **Secretary of Exordio Toastmasters Club, VIT (Fall Term 2026–27)**. This repository serves as a centralized archive for official Executive Committee (ExCom) Minutes of Meetings (MoM), club records, and leadership governance documentation.

---

## 📌 About the Role (Toastmasters International)

As **Secretary**, the primary responsibility is to maintain all club records, manage club files, handle club correspondence, and accurately record minutes of each club and executive committee meeting. The Secretary also manages membership records, keeps officer lists current with Toastmasters International, and upholds standard parliamentary procedures.

### Core Responsibilities
- **Meeting Minutes:** Record and document detailed minutes at all club and Executive Committee meetings.
- **Records & Archive Management:** Systematically organize, maintain, and archive club documents and historical records.
- **Membership & Rosters:** Maintain and distribute rosters of current paid members, keeping the club officer list current with Toastmasters International.
- **Parliamentary Compliance:** Understand and uphold basic parliamentary procedures and standard Toastmasters guidelines.
- **Correspondence & Logistics:** Handle club correspondence, remain current with the *Leader Letter*, and order club supplies as needed.

### Skills Developed
- **Organization & Record Maintenance:** Managing official documentation and systematic record-keeping.
- **Critical Thinking & Problem-Solving:** Identifying key action items and resolving operational challenges.
- **Decision Documentation & Communication:** Accurately documenting committee decisions and effectively communicating them to larger audiences.
- **Compliance & Governance:** Aligning club activities with Toastmasters International standards and procedures.
